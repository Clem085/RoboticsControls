## 2) Plant and PD architecture

**Plant (force \(u\) �+' position \(z\))**  
$$
P(s) \equiv \frac{Z(s)}{U(s)} = \frac{1}{m s^2 + b s + k},\quad m>0,\ b\ge 0,\ k>0.
$$

**PD with derivative on the output** (Fig. 7�?`2 style):  
$$

$$

In Laplace form (zero ICs): \(U(s) = k_P\,(Z_r - Z) - k_D\,s\,Z\).

## 3) Closed�?`loop transfer function \(T(s)=\dfrac{Z}{Z_r}\)

From \(Z = P\,[\,k_P Z_r - (k_P + k_D s) Z\,]\) we get
$$
Z\big(1 + P(k_P + k_D s)\big) = P\,k_P Z_r
\quad\Rightarrow\quad
T(s)=\frac{P\,k_P}{\,1 + P(k_P + k_D s)\,}.
$$

With \(P(s)=\dfrac{1}{m s^2 + b s + k}\),
$$
T(s)=\frac{k_P}{m s^2 + (b + k_D)s + (k + k_P)}.
$$

So the **closed�?`loop characteristic polynomial** is
$$
\Phi_{\text{cl}}(s) = m s^2 + (b + k_D)s + (k + k_P).
$$

## 4) Pole�?`placement formulas

### Option A �?" Specify \(\zeta\) and \(\omega_n\)
Target the standard second�?`order form \(s^2 + 2\zeta\omega_n s + \omega_n^2\).  
Match coefficients:
$$
\begin{aligned}
m s^2 + (b + k_D)s + (k + k_P) &= m\Big(s^2 + 2\zeta\omega_n s + \omega_n^2\Big) \\[4pt]
\Rightarrow\quad
k_D &= 2 m \zeta \omega_n \;-\; b, \\
k_P &= m \omega_n^2 \;-\; k.
\end{aligned}
$$

### Option B �?" Place a **double real pole** at \(s=p\) (with \(p<0\))
Target \((s - p)^2 = s^2 - 2 p s + p^2\).  
Match coefficients:
$$
\begin{aligned}
k_D &= -\,2 m p \;-\; b,\\
k_P &= m p^2 \;-\; k.
\end{aligned}
$$

### Option C �?" Two distinct poles \(s = p_{1,2}\)
Target \(s^2 - (p_1 + p_2)s + p_1 p_2\).  
Match coefficients:
$$
\begin{aligned}
k_D &= -\,m (p_1 + p_2) \;-\; b,\\
k_P &= m\,p_1 p_2 \;-\; k.
\end{aligned}
$$
