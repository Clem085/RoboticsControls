## 1) Problem
Linearize the planar VTOL altitude dynamics about hover and design a PD controller (derivative on the **output**) to place the closed�?`loop poles at desired locations. Provide the control law including the required equilibrium thrust and the closed�?`loop transfer function.

---

## 2) Altitude model about hover

For small pitch near hover, the vertical (altitude) dynamics reduce to a double�?`integrator with mass \(m\) after trimming out gravity. Let \(u\) be the total upward thrust. The physical equation is
$$
m\ddot z = -u + m g + d_z,
$$
where \(d_z\) is a lumped disturbance (unmodeled forces).

Trim at hover with \(u_{\text{eq}} = m g\). Define the **deviation input** \(u'(t) \equiv u(t) - u_{\text{eq}}\). Then
$$
m\ddot z = -\,u'(t) \quad\Rightarrow\quad
P(s) \equiv \frac{Z(s)}{U'(s)} = \frac{-1}{m s^2}.
$$

We can absorb the sign by redefining \(v \equiv -u'\) so that
$$
m\ddot z = v,\qquad \frac{Z}{V} = \frac{1}{m s^2}.
$$
Below we design \(v\) and then set \(u = u_{\text{eq}} - v\).

---

## 3) PD with derivative on the output

Use the deviation�?`signal form with \(v\) as the plant input:
$$
v(t) = k_P\big(z_r(t) - z(t)\big)\;-\;k_D\,\dot z(t).
$$

With \(P(s)=\dfrac{1}{m s^2}\), the closed�?`loop transfer function is
$$
T(s) \equiv \frac{Z}{Z_r} = \frac{k_P}{m s^2 + k_D s + k_P},
$$
so the closed�?`loop characteristic polynomial is
$$
\Phi_{\text{cl}}(s) = m s^2 + k_D s + k_P.
$$

---

## 4) Pole�?`placement formulas

### Option A �?" Specify \(\zeta,\ \omega_n\)
Match to \(m\big(s^2 + 2\zeta\omega_n s + \omega_n^2\big)\):
$$
k_D = 2 m \zeta \omega_n,\qquad
k_P = m \omega_n^2.
$$

### Option B �?" Double real pole at \(s=p\) (with \(p<0\))
$$
k_D = -\,2 m p,\qquad
k_P = m p^2.
$$

### Option C �?" Distinct poles \(s=p_{1,2}\)
$$
k_D = -\,m (p_1 + p_2),\qquad
k_P = m\,p_1 p_2.
$$

---

## 5) Full control law (with hover trim)
Recall \(v = k_P (z_r - z) - k_D \dot z\) and \(u = u_{\text{eq}} - v\) with \(u_{\text{eq}}=mg\). Thus
$$
\boxed{\,u(t) = m g \;-\; k_P\big(z_r(t) - z(t)\big) \;+\; k_D\,\dot z(t)\,}
$$
or, equivalently (sign�?`flipped \(v\) convention),
$$
\boxed{\,u(t) = m g \;+\; k_P\big(z_r(t) - z(t)\big) \;-\; k_D\,\dot z(t)\,}
$$
provided you keep the plant sign consistent with your definition. Either yields the same closed�?`loop polynomial \(m s^2 + k_D s + k_P\).

## 6) Notes for implementation
- If \(\dot z\) is noisy, use a filtered differentiator (e.g., \(\dot z_{\text{est}} = \frac{\alpha s}{s+\alpha}\,z\)).  
- Command limits: enforce \(u_{\min}\le u \le u_{\max}\) and retune \(\omega_n,\zeta\) to avoid wind�?`up.  
- Disturbance rejection: constant disturbances are rejected to a steady�?`state error of zero for steps in \(z_r\) only if you add integral action. Pure PD gives a non�?`zero bias if the trim is off; keep \(u_{\text{eq}}=mg\) accurate.
